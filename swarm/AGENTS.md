# Overnight swarm rules (hard limits)
You run unattended. Make your own decisions, but never:
1. Spend money: no paid APIs, no cloud resources, no purchases, no signups.
2. Contact outside people: no email, chat, issues, PRs, comments, or any message to anyone.
3. Push, publish, or deploy anything. Commit only to your own `swarm/*` branch in your own worktree.
4. Touch files outside your worktree.
If a task seems to need one of these, write it to `NEEDS_HUMAN.md` in your worktree and move on.
Commit small and often. When done, write a short `REPORT.md`.
